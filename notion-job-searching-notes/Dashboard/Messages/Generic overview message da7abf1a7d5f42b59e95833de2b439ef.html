Growth:

  • Tell me about a time where you failed.

I remember a time when I worked in a company in a cross-functional squad where we were very focused on execution, development and user interface design, leaving little time [or not time at all] to perform user experience work, such as user tasks and flows.

This lack of UX work had an expensive cost at the end of a release cycle, where we develop a blog post feature without taking in consideration the "Edit" option, that could be mapped and easily solved using a task flow.

After this situation the lesson I learned was not to skip important design phases because of the time, instead adapt the deliverables wthe the time available, to cover all the needs in different stages and fidelities, creating artifacts even in a simple way, to avoid future problems.

Leadership / Management:

  • How do you think about excellence as a design leader?

For me, leadership is about Influence, and an excellent design leader is the one who can influence people, teams, initiatives, and roadmaps with evidence that backed up the decisions made, explaining the reasons for what should be done or was done. At the same time, he or she should provide enough room for others to jump in and express what they feel about it and provide health feedback/comments to help move forward and improve.

  • What makes a designer excellent?

The best designers I know are the ones that are great listeners! I saw a strong correlation of hearing and understanding, with being a fantastic designer.

I believe that it is very hard to design something that you don't understand or not make sense for you, so based on that most of the time I tried to have a beginner mindset, allowing me to be in a position of learning, before designing solutions.

  • Tell me about your experiences with leading and managing.

Currently I'm leading the product design guild in Bitso, which is a side project where I had the challenge to define the discipline inside the company together with a group of 16 product designers.

In this initiative I take a facilitator role to empower the other designers during co-creation workshops sessions, and manage async initiatives to continue the work living outside the sessions.

My biggest challenge was to keep everyone motivated and engaged with this extra initiative, which was very time-consuming for me but at the same time very rewarding, to play a proactive role and get in contact with everyone, create deep relationships, and manage their perspective and ideas individually towards the whole group goals.
