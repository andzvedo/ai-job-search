Critique/Feedback

  • One of your students, brand new to design, just submitted their first draft of an e-commerce wireframe here. What feedback would you provide this student? (Please write your response as if a student was receiving your feedback.)

    I had the chance to receive feedback in many different ways, and the best ones that helped me move forward and improve my work were given without personal bias, which is what I do when I give feedback. I always start understanding the context, problem, and objectives to have enough information to evaluate work coming from others, and discuss the Designs in detail, avoiding personal biases. In this student example I would do something like this:

    Hello,

    Thank you for sending me the wireframe draft. How did you feel about it? It's looking cool!

    I'd love to understand better what were your motivations behind the decisions you made to come up with this structure.

    In this meantime I'd like to share some suggestions I had about it, and them we can discuss the ones that you think make more sense.

    I'm noticing that this wireframe represents a Product/Item page of an e-commence, and I saw that there is a "Back” button below the "Selected item image”, which I'm assuming it would get the users back to the last paged visited (please, correct me if I understood wrong).

    I would suggest changing the position of the "Back” button and positioning it right below the main navigation, in your case, below the clickable items ”Beds, Linens, Foundations, Sale, and All departments”, since the “Back” is part of the navigation.

    A good practice in e-commerces that is related to this, is to have a ”Breadcrumb” on product pages. Those Breadcrumbs are an excellent way to show users where they are located in the site. In your example a breadcrumb would be something like this:

    Initial page > Main category > Selected Item

    And also would positioned right below the main navigation.

    So to wrap it up:

    You can go with the “Back” button when a user lands a product page by the Search results, allowing them to go back to the search results, and use the Breadcrumb when a user accesses a product page from browsing.

    Does that make sense to you? Any questions, comments, or concerns about it?

    I'm looking forward to hearing more from you soon, and let's keep up Designing.

    All the best,

    André
