    Product Design Guild in Bitso

    I'm coordinating the Product Design Guild initiatives in Bitso.

    A guild is a side project where I had the challenge to define the discipline inside the company together with a group of 16 product designers.

    The first idea was to have a space to discuss the Product Design discipline, but we didn't have any objectives or goals set.

    So I thought in transform these meetings into a workshops session, to start exploring in a more collaborative way, what are the current situation for the PD perspectives and, based on that, what we should focus on. With the attempt to give a voice to everyone and the desire to create a safe space as well.

    Everything was very organic and passing the time I was iterating the ideas based on the feedback/ suggestions of other Product designers.

    Currently, we divided the team into four small groups trying to work on some specifics topics that the product designers mapped and voted as the most complex pains they were facing at the moment and creating some experiments that could improve the current situation in any way.

    And the desired outcome that we want to achieve by the end of the year is to have the learnings of this journey transformed into some basic guidelines of how the Product Designers could face these topics to avoid having the same pain again in the future.
