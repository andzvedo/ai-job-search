Overview of my professional skills

I'm a very curious Designer from Brazil.

ÁREAS

Design Research and Service Design: Understanding the challenge scenario

Working to understand the scenario of the challenge in question, from the strategic point of view and the needs of the company, as well as the main stakeholders and mainly by customers.

It is possible to map the front-stage and the back-stage of the service/product, make tangible the processes and procedures of the company, highlighting the opportunities and points of contacts existing with customers/consumers through the users ' journey and Identifying the frictions, greater pains, and opportunities for innovation.

Examples of deliverables of this phase:

Ethnographic research, in-depth interviews, Service Blueprint, Personas, User Journey, Co-creation Workshop.

Interaction Design: Drawing product/service touch points

After understanding the scenario it is possible to work on each touch point, drawing the structure of all the elements that compose the user experience and new ways of how this touch point will interact with the user, either through a service/ Digital product: Software, app or website, or through something physical, such as a totem, a printed catalog or a point of sale.

Examples of deliverables of this phase:

Information architecture, taxonomy, wireframes, task flow, usage scenarios, user interview, Low-fidelity interactive prototypes, and user testing.

Visual Design: Consistency and "Look and Feel "

After thinking about the interaction design, comes the time of the Visual design layer and translate into how the branding and tone of voice existing in the brand of the company will extend over the service/product, to give life to all the learning and the structure created. Creating the graphical interface and all the elements that compose it, thinking of typography, colors, Layout, icons, images beyond all that is important to contribute to the construction of meaning and understanding to users, to communicate without losing consistency with the Brand. Adapting to the different types of media and devices that exist today, such as desktop, mobile phones and also printed media, if applicable.

Examples of deliverables of this phase:

Graphical Interface and all the Visual part of the service/digital product or not, interactive high fidelity prototype, usability test

In all areas I working very hard to understand the people, their greatest anguish and difficulties during the day-to-day and how to translate the insights collected in a product/ Service that make sense to them and can also solve the company's needs and problems.

I also act in facilitating Design Thinking and Design Sprint sessions, such as Co-creation and prototyping Workshops.
