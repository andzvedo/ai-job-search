Why Wysefit?

I saw on Angelist that Wysefit is looking for a Head of Product & Design. It caught my attention because, as a Designer who co-founded a Gym in Brazil, called Treee Wellness (that the main focus is in Wellness and not only Fitness, with the majority of clients being middle-aged women) I think that I could help Wysefit to execute the vision of creating a global fitness and wellness brand for the older audience.

I love the idea behind Wysefit and I know how difficult it is to start a company from the ground and how hard it is to help people to keep active and exercise.

Talking about a digital solution to help Elder people? We can add a layer of complexity, let me highlight some topics:

- Elder people need extra care and attention

- There are topics related to injuries, Elder people are very sensitive

- Usually, they aren't so familiarized with technology

But, as a counterpoint, older people are much more faithful and resilient, which became a great opportunity to work with.
