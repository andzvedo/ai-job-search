Design Process

Process Blackbird.ai

Questions

  • What are the key topics that every design work should cover and have it documented?
    • Goal
    • Assumptions/Hypothesis
    • User needs
    • Proposal
  • What are the other important topics that the design work should cover depending of the initiative needs?

Keep in mind

  • The design documentation will vary depending of:
    • The initiative type and goals;
    • The level of knows and unknowns;
      • A new feature probably has more unknowns then an iteration of a existing feature
  • Keep in mind that the audience may vary, at the beginning of an initiative would be the Product team, and after that would be the engineering team.
    • Discovery: Product
      • Give context;
      • Informed design decisions;
    • Delivery: Engineers

To-dos

  • Take a look at the Product documents and raise questions;
  • What are the success metrics;
  • Create the Wireflows to start to make tangible the Flows in Miro and Minimum aspects
  • Align with the team

Goals

Be sure to cover what is the minimum that it needs from the Users, Business, and Technology perspective to be ready for launch.

Hypothesis

Define the Hypothesis that will be challenging the MVP

Priorities

Set the priorities to highlight what features should be tackled first and the reasons of it.

  • What’s the most important thing we need to learn first?
    • Why do we need to learn that?
  • What would be the estimated effort?
    • What are the risks involved?
    • What are the dependencies?
  • What’s the least amount of work to learn the next most important thing?
    • Be mindful of: Users, Business, and Technology perspectives

Discovery or Delivery focus

There are more Unknowns - Focus on Discovery

  • Collaborative ways to involve people that will help us get more clarity and seek the opportunities involved;

There are more Knowns - Focus on Delivery

  • Co-creation sessions with a specific problem and potential solution with others;

Research

What are the gray areas which it needs more clarity from the user's perspective?

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • In-depth studies
  • Concept Testing

Solution

Ideation

  • What if scenarios and brainstorm sessions

Flows and Features

Have something tangible, like a Wireframes, to align with the others from the team.

Ideal state

Define the ideal state with the vision of what is the desire outcome.

Must-have and Nice to have

Define the Flows and Features that are must-have and the nice to have ones.

  • Must-Have (To launch)

Be clear what are the reasons these features are considered Must-have.

  • What are the minimum requirements for these features?
  • What do we not know about them?
    • Enough knowledge around a feature > Ready to start the “Delivery”
  • What do we need/want to learn from our users that these features could provide?

  • Nice to have (Improvements)

Raise what are the nice to have features to be aware of.

Collect feedback

  • Product team feedback
  • Engineers feedback

Success Metrics

Define what should be measure, that will show the impact of the solution, helping to understand what is working and what's not.

  • What will be the success criteria of the features?
    • Business metrics
    • UX metrics
  • How will be measured?
  • What is the minimum amount of effort of something that should be built to generate learning?
