The Brief

ABOUT THE CLIENT

Nomart is an itinerant exposition that showcases the art of local artists in developing communities. Its main business objective is to enhance the visibility of artists that don’t have the resources or appeal to get exposed in the current art gallery scene.

PROJECT SUMMARY

Nomart requires a progressive web app. This app should help both the art curators and artists to create virtual exhibitions for people that cannot travel to see the exhibitions in person. The solution should be playful and informative, giving the user an interactive experience whilst helping artists, critics, and curators connect and update their exhibitions on the go.

Create an initial pitch presentation containing the rationale behind your design decisions as well as any documentation you see fit to support your ideas.

TASKS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

As a UX designer, your task is to Demonstrate strategic approach in your design process as well as apply industry-standard methodologies to understand the wider context, frame the problem, and produce assets that will empower UI designers to create functional, usable, and delightful user experience. Make sure you provide strong rationale behind your design decisions in your presentation so that stakeholder can understand and approve your work.
