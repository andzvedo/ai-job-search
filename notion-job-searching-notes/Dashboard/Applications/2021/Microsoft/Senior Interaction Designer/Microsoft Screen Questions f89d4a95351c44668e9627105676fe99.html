  • Leadership / Management:

    How do you think about excellence as a design leader? 

    For me leadership is about Influence, and an excellent design leader is the one who can influence people, teams, initiatives and roadmaps, with evidences that backed up the decisions made, explaining the reasons of what something should be done, or was done, but at the same time, provide enough room for the others jump in and express what they fell about it, and provide health feedbacks/comments to help to move forward and improve.

    What makes a designer excellent?

    The best designers I know are the ones that are great listeners! I saw a strong correlation of hearing and understand, with to be an awesome designer.

    I believe that is very hard to design something that you don't understand or not make sense for you, so based on that most of the time I tried to have a beginner mindset, allowing me to be in a position of learning, before design solutions.

    Tell me about your experiences with leading and managing.

    Current I'm leading the product design guild in Bitso, which is a side project where I had the challenge to define the discipline inside the company together with a group of 16 product designers.

    In this initiative I take a facilitator role to empower the others designers during co-creation workshops sessions, and managing async initiatives to continue the work living outside the sessions.

    My biggest challenge was to keep everyone motivated and engaged with this extra initiative, which was very time consuming for me but at the same time very rewarding, to play a pro-active role and get in contact with everyone, create deeply relationships, and manage theirs perspective and ideas individually towards the whole group goals.
