About the company

To make this project I worked at a company called Magazine Luiza, which is one of the biggest retailer companies in Brazil. They have more them 1000 physical stores throughout the Brazil territory and a strong online presence with their e-commerce.

Business Goal

One of the most important and ambitious goals for Magalu at the time was "Help Brazilian companies to digitize themselves". Based on this goal, the business people and the management team from Magalu saw the opportunity to offer their own services to other retail stores.

Outcome

The outcome of this project was to create a solution where it was possible for other e-commerces to contract retail physical services online, initially offered by Magalu, and at some point, in the future, they could have the possibility to offer their services on the platform as well.

Backup

The outcome of this project was to create a solution where would be possible for other e-commerces to hire physical services online, initially offered by Magalu, and sometime in the future could have the possibility for e-commerces to offer their services as well.

The outcome of this project was to create a solution that allows other retail stores to hire - or sell - physical services online, initially offered by Magalu, and manage users and their permissions, technical documentation and APIs integration, and payments in a seamless experience, something similar to what Firebase did for cloud services, but for retail services.

that will allow Magazine Luiza and other retail stores to sell digital or physical services online (free services too) to small retailers integrated on their own e-commerce, paying based on the demand used, like Firebase and Google Cloud do it for cloud services.

My role

As a UX/UI Designer, I was responsible to design from scratch the solution for it, working alongside a very technical team, composed of a Teach Lead, a QA engineer, four Front-end developers, and two Back-end developers, and a Product owner.

It's important to express business requirements in different ways

  • Understand the company strategy, goals, and expected outcomes to have a clear direction of the path we must walk through and not focus on some requirements.

    The Business requirements come in different shapes and sizes, and these requirements (most of the time) are not focused on the user needs, but on the company perspective, and is my responsibility as a Designer to translate these requirements into something that generate impact for users and is possible to build in the desired time.

  • Get real users involved in the design process and don't miss to explore the Problem space

This project was focused on the Solution space, with just less work of research, and too much interface work.

  • Being a Design Leader in every place I'm, providing opportunities to the company and team about Design itself.

    I didn't take the ownership and leadership as a Designer to show for the company and the team opportunities to explore the Problem space before jumping into the Solution space, with more knowledge and fewer assumptions, to have the opportunity to create a solution that really could bring value to people, and not just expend time and generating frustration of an entire team.

  • Understand the value of Agile, and how could be costly when not

    Working under a fake Agile, using the methods and the rituals, but designing and developing in a waterfall manner.

    Fake agile environment

    Lack of real agile environment

    No CI/CD environment

  • Embrace failure as part of building knowledge!

    Fail is part of the process, bringing knowledge to the people involved, but the failure needs to happen fast, using quick experiments, and validations with real users, without involving too much effort.

Because of the low design maturity of the company and the team, I was in, plus my lack of ownership and leadership, in a work based on assumptions!

No opportunity for research work, no exploration or validation, I didn't convince the team to invest time in understanding the problem space, before start designing a solution with better knowledge.

The result of a weak product, without market fit, that wasn't a hit

Involve stakeholders and other areas in the design process

  • Learned the value of collaboration with others from the team, stakeholders, and other people that could help increase the knowledge around a topic.

    As an introverted person, I preferred to do my work alone in isolation, and this project made me move around and be involved with the engineers to get alignment together.
