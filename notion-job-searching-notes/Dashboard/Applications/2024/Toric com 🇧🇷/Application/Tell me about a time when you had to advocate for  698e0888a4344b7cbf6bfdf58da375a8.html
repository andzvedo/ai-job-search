MaaS Ecosystem
  • During my time at Magazine Luiza, I worked on the MaaS Console project, where I had to mediate a disagreement between the Product Owner and the Tech Lead regarding the implementation of a complex service registration process. The Product Owner wanted a streamlined, minimalistic design, while the Tech Lead emphasized the need for detailed information to meet technical requirements. I facilitated a meeting where both parties could express their concerns and priorities. Using my understanding of both user-centered design and technical feasibility, I proposed a compromise: a design that maintained the streamlined user interface but included expandable sections where users could access the necessary technical details if needed. This approach satisfied both parties, leading to a smooth development process and a successful product launch ￼.

Have you led projects through an end-to-end design process: research, ideation, strategy, information architecture, interaction design, launch, and testing?

Yes, I have led several projects through the end-to-end design process. A good example of that is when I led the design of an automated onboarding process for enterprise institutions at Bitso, focusing on compliance and KYC. This project encompassed extensive user research to understand the needs and pain points of the onboarding process, ideation and strategy sessions to develop a streamlined user flow, and rigorous user testing to ensure the final product was both user-friendly and compliant with regulatory standards. It was also consistent with Bitso's Design system. I collaborated closely with Compliance, Product, and Engineering teams to ensure the final product met both user needs and business goals. After launch, I measured the impact of the solution with data usage with Amplitude, highlighting potential improvement areas that might need a new round of discovery and delivery from the design perspective.
