Dear UpKeep hiring team,

My design journey, fueled by a love for art and problem-solving, has led me to a career where creativity meets functionality. Combining a background in Graphic Design with User-Centered Design expertise, I can translate business goals into solutions that address real user needs.

My entire professional journey has been rooted in the B2B sector, where the distinct challenge of limited user accessibility has significantly impacted my approach to user research. To navigate this, I've cultivated strong partnerships with Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success teams. Their insights into market dynamics and customer behaviors have been pivotal, enabling me to conduct targeted and insightful research, turning limited access into deeper user insights.

This collaboration has refined my research techniques, making them more nuanced and focused. I blend direct interactions with users whenever possible, but also leverage indirect data analysis through analytics and team feedback. Such an integrated approach ensures that my design decisions are deeply aligned with user needs, navigating the B2B landscape's unique constraints effectively.

Consequently, my design process is thoroughly human-centered, adaptable, and crafted to deliver intuitive and impactful solutions that resonate with the complexities of B2B users.

In addition, the participation of Product and Engineering peers in the design process ensures a smooth integration of user needs into the product development process, which enhances alignment and fosters stronger bonds within the teams.

Beyond user flows, wireframes, high-fidelity and interactive prototypes for screen-based solutions, I champion a holistic approach informed by service design principles.

This includes mapping user pain points and needs to identify opportunities for maximizing value, a skill that aligns perfectly with UpKeep's focus on optimizing operational efficiency.

One past experience that I'd love to bring here was when I worked with Novidá. In 2019, as a Freelance Product Designer for Novidá, a Brazilian SaaS startup focused on operational team oversight, I redesigned their web and mobile app to enhance managerial capabilities. This experience directly aligns with UpKeep's mission to revolutionize maintenance and facility management through technology.

I'm eager to learn more about UpKeep and how my skills in user research, user-centered design, and cross-functional collaboration can contribute to your continued growth and success.

Please don't hesitate to reach out if you'd like to schedule a call, explore additional materials, or have any questions.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
André Azevedo
