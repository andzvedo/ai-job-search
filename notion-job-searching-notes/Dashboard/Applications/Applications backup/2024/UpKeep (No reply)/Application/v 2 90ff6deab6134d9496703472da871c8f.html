Overall Professional and Design experience

I have extensive experience in Product Design, Service design and UX Research, primarily in the B2B sector across various industries. My international design experience spans more than six sectors, including consultancy, retail, fintech, edtech, artificial intelligence, and business intelligence, in companies from Brazil, Mexico, and the United States.

In my career, I've worked with numerous startups such as Blackbird, Bitso, SIA, and Arquivei, and supported early-stage startups as a freelancer, notably Novidá. This has provided me with a broad perspective on design challenges, and the agility to navigate them effectively.

My design process is rooted in human-centered design, using an evidence-based approach. I design products from scratch, collaborating with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. To inform my designs, I gather data through generative and evaluative research studies like user interviews, UX analysis, concept testing, and usability testing. I also use tools like Hotjar, Mixpanel, or Amplitude for real usage data analysis.

I adopt a holistic approach from service design to map out customer pain-points and needs, and identifying opportunities beyond screen-specific solutions. I think that this approach can be useful for UpKeep, given the range of the solutions provided, from the SaaS, to Industrial IoT sensors, data analytics tools, advanced enterprise integrations, and professional services.

Lastly, I believe that cross-functional collaboration with teams like Customer Success, Sales, Marketing, Product, and Engineering, is key to create effective design solutions.
