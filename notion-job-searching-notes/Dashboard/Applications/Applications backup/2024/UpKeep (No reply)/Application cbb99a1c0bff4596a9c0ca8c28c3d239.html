1. Cover letter

André Azevedo

Brazilian Product Designer

Dear UpKeep Hiring Team,

I am thrilled at the opportunity to apply for the Senior UX Designer position at UpKeep.

My love for art and problem-solving has led me to a career where I can combine creativity with functionality. With a background in Graphic Design and expertise in User-Centered Design, I can turn business goals into solutions that solve real user problems.

My entire career has been in the B2B sector, where the challenge of limited user access has shaped my approach to user research. To overcome this, I've built strong relationships with Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success teams. Their insights into market trends and customer behavior have been crucial, allowing me to conduct targeted and insightful research. This turns limited access into a deeper understanding of user needs.

Working with these teams has made my research methods more detailed and focused. I combine direct interaction with users, whenever possible, with indirect data analysis from reports and team feedback. This combined approach ensures my design choices closely match user needs, even within the unique challenges of the B2B world.

As a result, my design process is all about the user, can be adapted, and is built to deliver easy-to-use and effective solutions that work well for complex B2B users.

In addition to user flows, wireframes, and high-quality interactive prototypes, I also believe in a more holistic approach based on service design principles. This means visualizing user journeys to find ways to create integrated and valuable experiences across different channels and touch-points. I believe that this skill perfectly aligns with UpKeep's focus on improving operational efficiency.

One experience I'd love to bring to UpKeep is from my time as a Freelance Product Designer at Novidá in 2019. Novidá is a Brazilian SaaS startup focused on helping operational teams. I redesigned their web and mobile app to make it easier for managers to do their jobs. This experience directly connects to UpKeep's mission of using technology to revolutionize maintenance and facility management.

I'm excited to learn more about UpKeep and how my skills in user-centered design, user research, and working with different teams can help you continue to grow and succeed.

Please don't hesitate to contact me if you'd like to schedule a call, share additional information, or have any questions.

Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,

André Azevedo

The chance to enhance processes, shape the product design discipline, and build a Product Design team at UpKeep aligns perfectly with my skillset and passion. This opportunity to leverage my experience in a company with a clear vision for the future mirrors my time leading the Product Design Guild at Bitso. Beyond the team and process aspects, I'm genuinely motivated by the impact UpKeep's product has. Working on something that empowers blue-collar workers and improves efficiency in maintenance and facilities is both challenging and rewarding.

Overall, UpKeep offers a fantastic opportunity to leverage my experience, contribute to a meaningful product, and join a thriving company culture.
