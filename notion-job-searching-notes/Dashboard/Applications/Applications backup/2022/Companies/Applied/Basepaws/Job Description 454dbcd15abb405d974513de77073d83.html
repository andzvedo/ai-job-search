Job Description

Product Design Lead, full-time - Basepaws

What You’ll Do:

As a Product Design Lead with Basepaws, you’ll lead both the strategy and execution of our digital user experience, owning the end-to-end vision for our user flows throughout our e-commerce platform to our digital ecosystem. As the first in-house Product Design Lead, you’ll be responsible for creating and implementing our design systems, bringing our refreshed brand to life digitally, performing and analyzing research to deeply understand the rapidly evolving, modern pet consumer (parent and veterinarian), and designing the future intersection of genetics, precision healthcare, and consumer technology through our user experience, continuously raising the bar for how we approach design at Basepaws.

This role reports directly to the Head of Product, and works in close partnership with Product Managers, Data, and Engineering.

Who You Are:

You’re as passionate about designing intuitive user solutions as you are about pets and the humans who care for them. You’re a constant advocate for your end-user, framing problems with their needs in mind. You like to design in spaces where no playbook exists, are excited by ambitious goals, and won’t settle for the status quo. You see as much value in iterative, data-driven roadmaps as you do customer interviews and product feedback. You are obsessed with consumer needs and motivations and aren’t afraid to get scrappy to learn.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Represent Design throughout the Product Development lifecycle, including [EVERYTHING - ONE PERSON TEAM:]
    • User research,
    • Competitive analysis
    • Conceptual design
    • User flow definition and information architecture,
    • Interaction design
    • UX writing
    • Visual design.
    • Deliver all aspects of project from concept, ideation, exploration, wireframe, prototyping, visual design and launch materials.
  • Partner with Product to define and evolve our digital product offering roadmap, strategy and vision.
  • Participate in decisions around scope, sequencing and phasing of product launches, and technical discussions with Engineering as needed, in partnership with Product.
  • Define and measure success customer experience metrics for designs (in partnership with Data and Product) pre and post launch.
  • Serve as the Product Design expert for a talented group of marketers, product managers, scientists, and engineers.
  • Actively seek and action feedback from peers and customers to improve the experience for our community of pet parents and veterinarians.

Qualifications:

  • 5+ years of experience as UX/Product Designer, past experience in an early start-up a major plus
  • Demonstrated experience with design tools, such as Figma, Sketch and /or other prototyping tools
  • Comfort with early-stage Product teams and excited by the opportunity to create our long-term digital vision
  • Very comfortable bringing together stakeholders with varied perspectives (science, marketing, operations, etc) to align goals, understand differences, and explore solutions efficiently
  • Depth of experience working and collaborating with creative thinkers across Product, Marketing, and Design
  • Past experience with data-driven design and goaling your work through business, product and UX metrics
  • Comfortable defining research objectives, participating in user research studies analyzing the results, and summarizing learnings for a broader group
  • Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to manage expectations, align differing perspectives, and action constructive criticism
  • Portfolio demonstrating a past history of shipping high impact consumer products
  • Bachelor's degree in human-computer interaction, user experience or related design field, or equivalent related professional and/or bootcamp experience
  • A solutions-oriented, collaborative mindset, who is receptive to feedback and able to laugh willingness to identify opportunities and bring solutions to the teams in which you work
  • Experience with (or a willingness to rapidly learn) the following design platforms: web, mobile web and native apps

Please be ready to submit your portfolio upon request.
