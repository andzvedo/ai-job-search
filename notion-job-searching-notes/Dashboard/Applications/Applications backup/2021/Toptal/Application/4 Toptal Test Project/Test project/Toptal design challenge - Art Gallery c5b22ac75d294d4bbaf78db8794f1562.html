Toptal design challenge - Art Gallery

Briefing notes

About Business

Nomart is an itinerant exposition that showcases the art of local artists in developing communities.

Business objective

Enhance the visibility of artists that don’t have the resources or appeal to get exposed in the current art gallery scene.

Expected Output

Design a progressive web app for a Mixed Reality art gallery

Needs

Create (update and manage) virtual exhibitions for people that cannot travel to see the exhibitions in person;

Enable connections between the actors involved with an exhibition;

With an interactive playful, and informative experience.

Design Process

Double Diamond Adaptation

Following the Double Diamond framework and Human-Centered Design approach, understanding the different types of people/actors involved in the project scenario to Design for them.

Phases

  • Research - Problem Space (Discovery and Define)
  • Design - Solution Space (Develop and Delivery)

Research [Problem space]

Current art gallery scenario

To understand the context of the business, and the main challenges they are facing, to prioritize what problem would be solved next.

What is an Art Gallery?

  • An art gallery is an exhibition space for the display and sale of artworks.
    • Is a commercial enterprise working with a portfolio of artists that acts as the dealer representing and distributing the artworks by the artists?
  • They are the link between artists and collectors
  • They are always scouting for new talent to showcase the best artists with their exhibition program.

What do Art Galleries do?

  • Creates an Exhibition program
    • Exhibition program will be the core of the identity of the gallery for a specific period of time, in general six to eight weeks).
  • Provides a physical space for the exhibition to happen
    • Most often, galleries rent commercial properties with large windows in big cities – often in close proximity of other galleries – to have great visibility towards many potential clients.
  • Scouting for artists
    • Curates their program scouting for the most interesting emerging artists, and inviting them to participate in the exposition.
  •  Building a portfolio of artists
    • Build a portfolio of artists which is carefully curated selection of artists the gallery commits to represent, consisting of a long-term collaboration between the artist and the gallery. We call these long-term collaborations represented artists, whereas artists from a one time collaboration for one specific exhibition are referred to as exhibited artists.
  • Sell and distribute artworks
    • Sell and distribute the artworks from the artists the gallery represents;
    • And also take care of everything related with the exhibition, like transportation, insurance, supervision, installation, print work, press interest, invitations, opening and promotion of the exhibition.

Source: https://www.contemporaryartissue.com/the-art-gallery-everything-you-need-to-know/

  • People who want to buy art;

Local artists in developing communities

To understand what are the main needs of Local artists from developing communities within the Art scene.

What are the challenges they face and their main needs?

  • Present new forms of representation,
  • Invest in strengthening the territory,
  • Promote dialogue and protagonist of residents
  • Help to bring culture for their communities, and increase the Social Progress

How usually these artists present their work?

  • Usually they present their work in person at the communities they live.
  • And use Instagram (or facebook) as their online portfolio.

Source: https://observatoriodefavelas.org.br/arte-favela-e-resistencia/ (In Portuguese)

The Problem

The Art galleries business is focused on making money from Artworks, and they choose Artists to collaborate with that are usually well known. They usually don't focus on new artists or the ones that are not very well recognized.

For those artists to get into those galleries, it's almost impossible, so they focus on exposing artwork in their local communities, to give back to their people a bit of culture.

Opportunity

Be the Art Gallery chosen by Local Artists to represent them, giving the exposure they are looking for in their people and communities through their Artwork, helping them achieve a broader audience that sees value not only in the work but also in the artist behind that piece of art.

Design [Solution space]

How might we …

  • … find those local artists that are interested in participate in Nomart exhibitions?
  • … be attractive enough for those artists get in contact with Nomart curators?

Hypothesis

I believe that creating a way for Artists being able to expose not only their art as an individual but also the community they live could help them achieve more things (such as bring attention to people outside their communities of the issues they face in the day to day as artists), and became more attractive encouraging them to be part of it.

Proposal

An Art Ecosystem composed of Artist Portfolio, Gallery, and Exhibitions that connects local communities’, and their artists, with a global audience.

Main areas

Artists Portfolio

Improve the Local Artist's discoverability by Art Curators

  • An online Portfolio for Local Artists to show their work and community and get connected with other artists, curators, and art collectors.

Gallery

Be the place where Art Collectors and art enthusiasts go to get in contact with new art and artists

  • A Gallery that showcases the Artwork from Artist's portfolios.

Exhibitions

Events that can showcase on another level the Artwork, Artists, and also their Communities to create a bond with the public

  • Exhibitions that show various mediums of artwork and the behind-the-scenes, or any other material needed.

Focus
Until this moment, the focus was on having the big picture of the Project mapped out to help stakeholders, myself, and others from the team visualize opportunities, and be aligned with the current scenario of the project.

Create a new Exhibition

it's time to narrow the scope, focusing on how would the Creation of a New Exhibition would impact the Curators and Artists day-to-day, connecting the three main areas (Artist Portfolio, Gallery, and Exhibitions) into a Concept, that would allow the future to gather feedback from end users before investing more effort on it.

Basic requirements

  • Choose the Main Theme that will guide the identity of the Exhibition
  • Add Description of the Exhibition
  • Add the Artwork and Artists which will be part of the Exhibition
  • Publish the Exhibition

Next Steps

The next steps would be:

  • Do a Design review of the low fidelity proposal with Normart stakeholders to understand if the proposal goes in the same direction as the business objective;
  • Iterate based on their feedback together with the UI Designer and prepare a concept test to put into end users’ hands;

  • Iterate based on the feedback received and move to the high fidelity wireframes, pairing with the UI Designer.
