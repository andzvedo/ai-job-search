Senior Product Designer

🏔️ About Sierra Studio

Sierra Studio is an AI engineering studio and startup incubator – we turn our customers’ ideas into reality! We’re based in Los Angeles, but our teammates live all around the world. We create products across all areas of software — from web and mobile apps to cutting-edge work at the forefront of AI and large language models.

💼 About The Role

We’re hiring a senior product designer to create AI products that are easy to use, beautiful, and push the boundaries of what’s possible. You’ll help chart our course into the future as we evolve our technology, product, and design paradigms. As an early employee at a startup, you’ll also be part of creating our culture!

🔎 What you'll do in this role

  • Contribute to the overall design and product direction of our clients' products
  • Design and ship high-quality products, from early concepts to high-fidelity prototypes
  • Work closely with our clients and our team’s engineers and product managers
  • Conduct user research to better understand customer needs
  • Help to shape our design culture and grow our team

🔎 Who We’re Looking For

  • Have 5+ years of experience designing software products
  • Have a portfolio showcasing strong critical thinking and UX/UI skills
  • Excellent taste in products and deep curiosity to understand customer pain points
  • Can think through ambiguous design problems and shape them into a clear vision
  • Possess impactful communication and storytelling skills
  • Excited about defining how AI products should look and behave
  • Nice to have: frontend engineering skills (e.g. TypeScript, React)
  • Nice to have: experience designing AI products

💖 Our culture

  • Growth mindset: We look to magnify our strengths, take chances, and always learn from our mistakes.
  • Supportive: We keep our egos out of the workplace and support each other when trying new things.
  • Product & customer centric: We care deeply about our customers and clients and always keep their needs top of mind. We build tech to solve real problems.
  • Ownership: We take initiative, even when it is “not our job.” As a startup, we all wear lots of hats.

💡 Why join us?

At Sierra Studio, we believe in nurturing talent and creating good vibes at work. We seek inclusion and embrace our differences. We’re all about “growth mindset”, and we look to magnify our strengths, take chances, and always learn from our mistakes. We don’t fit our teammates into boxes, and instead we understand that each person has their own unique set of skills that they bring to the table. If you join us, we’ll embrace who you are today, and give you the space and tools to keep growing.

Design Challenge

Scenario

Hello! You’ve been hired by a startup to design a mobile app to compare
different LLMs. Their target audience is AI engineers who want to see how
different models perform on the domain area of their project.

Example use case

An AI engineer at iFood wants to compare OpenAI GPT-5.1 vs. Google
Gemini 3 Pro to see which has a better understanding of Italian cuisine.

Requirements

A user should be able to

  • Select which models they want to compare from a list
  • Chat with all models simultaneously
  • The user will form their opinion about the models through
    conversation (think “vibes” instead of quantitative metrics)
  • Configure the
    • system prompt,
    • temperature,
    • and other settings
      • Feel free to include other features that you think the user would want
  • When you’re done, please send us a Figma that includes:
    • Your designs for this product
    • Explanation of your process and why you made key design decisions
    • What you would improve further if this was a real product

Assessment

You’ll be assessed on these criteria:

  • Critical thinking: how did you make decisions?
  • Product sense: does the app solve the problem?
  • UX design: is the app intuitive?
  • Visual design: does the app look good?
  • Creativity: how innovative is the solution?
