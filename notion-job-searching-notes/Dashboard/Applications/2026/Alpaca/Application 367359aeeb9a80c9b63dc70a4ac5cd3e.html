My design process combines qualitative research, user feedback, usability testing, product usage data, and holistic methods.

At Blackbird.ai, I use Amplitude, session recordings, user feedback, and research insights to identify friction and improve data-heavy workflows.

One example was a project where users were confused by different data scopes between Analyze and Network Graph. By reviewing feedback, support tickets, and user behavior, I identified that the problem was a mental model mismatch. I helped redesign the flow by making the data logic clearer, improving naming, grouping metrics, adding visible data counts, and introducing a preview step before graph generation. This reduced related support tickets by 14% after the first month and 39% after the second month. You can see this work here: www.andreazevedo.design/work/blackbird

I also apply systemic and holistic service design methods to synthesize research results into journey maps, service blueprints, user flows, and opportunity areas. A good example is my Find Food case study, where I translated research findings into structured insights and design opportunities with a visual research wall artifact: www.andreazevedo.design/work/find-food

Overall, I use data to make product decisions more evidence-based and aligned with user needs and business goals.
