Please share your experience with AI or Generative AI features—whether through design, integration, or the use of AI-powered tools in your work.*

At Blackbird.ai, I design an AI-powered narrative intelligence platform. Recent work on Narrative Feed and Compass Vision involved mapping complex information retrieval and summarization flows, then iterating high-fidelity prototypes to validate how LLM outputs (text + visual signals) should be surfaced.

I also created a design system workshop using Cursor, Figma, Radix UI/shadcn/Tailwind, GitHub, and Vercel to make it both human- and AI-ready. It includes reference implementations with code snippets that accelerate engineering handoff, plus automatically generated natural-language prompts giving AI assistants the full context needed to implement design-system-compliant components.

In parallel, I run AI + Vision side projects. The best example is Imaginotation: a work-in-progress personal digital product that uses Gemini Vision to parse UI screenshots, auto-tag them, and store structured metadata in Supabase—demonstrating how multimodal models can drive knowledge organization.

Day to day, I use Builder.io, Lovable, and AI IDEs (Cursor, Windsurf, Amazon Kiro, Gemini CLI) to turn Figma concepts into interactive, data-instrumented prototypes in hours, while experimenting with MCP integrations that link Figma Dev Mode to back-end data. This AI-accelerated workflow lets me design, integrate, and evaluate generative features rapidly—collecting real usage feedback and iterating based on evidence.
