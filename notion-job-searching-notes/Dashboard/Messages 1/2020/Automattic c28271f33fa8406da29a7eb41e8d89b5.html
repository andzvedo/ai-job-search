WORDPRESS.COM UX DESIGNER APPLICATION LETTER

Tell us why you’d be a good fit for the role.

I´m a Brazilian Designer fan of remote work and I care a lot about diversity and help people get what they need with less friction possible. I have a background as a graphic designer but now I´m working as a Visual, Interaction and Service Designer creating digital and physical interfaces. Working at a platform that is responsible to more than 32% of the entire internet will be a great opportunity to put my full stack design knowledge to create a good impact on people lives helping Wordpress.com clients. Without to mention that I could bring to the Auttomatic Design team a more holistic design approach and diversity perspective of how to create solution more human-centered, based on research and not assumptions because of my generalist background, starting with a problem and finishing with a tested interface. And as a Designer Advocate could express the word of Automatic Design through all the events that I do or participate for the Brazilian Design community.

What is the UX design or product strategy accomplishment that you are most proud of?

I´m a Human Centered Designer so all my projects I started talked with real people, and a project that I´m very proud of was what I did for a Gym called Treee Wellness. Where I help than to developed an web based software and an a Mobile App for help their clients how to implement the physical exercise routines in your daily life. I could do this after research and understand that the most difficult aspect related to exercised for the Treee Wellness clients are the lack of time.

Tell us about a time where research you were involved in influenced the business or roadmap strategy. How did you make it happen?

I worked at a project to a big pharmaceutical industry, at a project very challenging, that I need to help the organization to understand the real problem and create and validate a solution. So I did an entire design process, start with understanding the business side and all the key stakeholders to generate the input to do and user research. With the insights and data analyzed from user research, was possible to show the organization that the real problem wasn´t what the expected, but a more deep problem. With the real problem in mind, I create a concept together with the company of an started to design a prototype of a solution. With the prototype I put it in the hands of the real users to collect feedback about the interface and how was the gaps that the solution have and how to improve. I´m very proud of this project because the health market is a very difficult and competitive market, where the users of the tests said "That solution that you are showing are so easy to use and will help my entire team to do a better job and improve the Brazilian hospital patients"
