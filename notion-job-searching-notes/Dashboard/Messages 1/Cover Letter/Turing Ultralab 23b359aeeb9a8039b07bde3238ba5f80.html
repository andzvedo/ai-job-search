Why UltraLab?

Turning frontier AI into testable product experiences—at speed—is exactly where I’ve been pushing my practice. I map complex workflows, then stand up interactive prototypes that feel real enough for user feedback and stakeholder conviction.

My AI-first prototyping toolkit

I start ideation in Figma, super-charging it with Stitch, Magic Path and Figma Make to explore flows quickly. When a concept is ready for interaction, I move to Lovable, Builder.io, v0.dev and Bolt.new to turn static frames into high-fidelity coded prototypes.

To polish and extend those prototypes into real web apps, I rely on AI-powered IDEs—Cursor, Windsurf, Amazon Kiro and the Gemini CLI—so refactors and feature spikes happen in minutes, not days. My core stack stays modern and developer-friendly: React/Next.js with Radix UI, shadcn/ui and Tailwind CSS on the front end, plus Node.js and Supabase/PostgreSQL for lightweight, data-capturing back ends.

I also experiment with multimodal AI, piping GPT-4o and Gemini Vision models into quick pipelines—for instance, Imaginotation, a side project that auto-tags UI screenshots by parsing them on the fly.

Together, these tools let me convert Figma concepts into data-instrumented, code-backed prototypes within a single workday—ready for user testing, actionable analytics and seamless hand-off to engineering.
